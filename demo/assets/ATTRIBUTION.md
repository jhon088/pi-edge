# Demo artwork — where it comes from

Every image in this folder comes from **`examples/fotos`**, the photography
supplied by the product owner for this demo. Nothing here was downloaded,
generated or substituted.

Each file is a centre crop of its source to 1:1, resized down to at most
1000×1000. No stretching, no recolouring, no retouching: the crop and the
scale are the only operations, and neither changes what is on the plate.

## Two things worth knowing

**`Helado de Limon.png` is chocolate ice cream**, not lemon. The filename is
wrong; the photograph is not. It is mapped to the chocolate dessert, which is
what it shows.

**`Coca Clasica.jpg` is cropped, not centred.** The source is a landscape
advertisement: the glass sits right of centre with the wordmark and ad copy
filling the left third. A centred square crop would have sliced the wordmark
down the middle. The crop starts to the right of the copy, so what is left is
the glass alone — a product shot, not an ad.
